👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Method

1

Marinate for Tenderness

Toss chicken pieces into the buttermilk and pickle juice brine with salt. Let rest for at least 30 minutes (or up to 4 hours in the fridge).

2

Double-Coat Dredge

Toss panko and flour with spices. Take chicken from marinade, press firmly into the crumb mix so every crevice is coated with crunchy flakes.

3

Cook to Golden Perfection

Air Fryer: Preheat to 390°F (200°C), spray lightly with oil, and cook for 10-12 minutes, shaking basket at halfway mark. (Or deep fry in 350°F oil for 4-5 minutes until golden brown).

4

Rest & Dip

Transfer to a cooling wire rack for 2 minutes to let the steam escape without softening the crust. Serve with your favorite dipping sauce flight!
